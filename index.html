FAQ

Can I turn multiple screenshots into one note?

Yes. Add screenshots on the same topic, then extract and merge them in the order you choose. You can use screenshots from slides, online classes, and web pages.

What if the extracted text or the final note needs a correction?

You can correct the text from each image and edit the final result before inserting it into your note. Compare it with the original when a screenshot is blurry or contains formulas.

Can I keep the screenshots and the finished note?

Yes. You can insert the result into a note and keep the screenshots as attachments. You can edit, search, and export the note as Markdown.
